SaveYourScreenTime
Privacy Policy

Your Screen Time data stays on your phone. That's the whole policy.

Last updated · September 16, 2026

SaveYourScreenTime helps you track your weekly Apple Screen Time totals over the long term. It has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics — everything you enter or import is stored only on your own device. This page explains exactly what that means.

01

What we collect

→

Nothing. SaveYourScreenTime doesn't have a server to send data to. No account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no ad network — none of that exists in the app.

We — the person who made this app — never see your Screen Time numbers, the names of apps you use, or anything else you enter. There is nothing for us to see: it never leaves your phone.

02

What's stored on your device

The app saves the following, locally, using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData) — the same mechanism Apple's own apps use to store data privately on your phone:

  • Weekly totals you enter manually or import from a screenshot
  • App and category names and durations you've logged or imported
  • Your Reduce / Neutral preferences for each app or category
  • Optional notes you write for a given week

None of this is backed up to iCloud, synced anywhere, or shared with any other app on your phone.

03

Screenshot imports

When you import a week from a Screen Time screenshot, the app reads the text in the image using Apple's on-device text recognition (Vision framework) — the same technology behind Live Text. This happens entirely on your phone; the image is never uploaded anywhere.

The photo's data exists in memory only for the moment it takes to read the text out of it — typically under a second — and is discarded immediately afterward. It is never written to disk, never kept in memory afterward, and never leaves your device. Only the numbers you review and confirm on the next screen — which you can edit before saving — are stored.

04

Third parties

SaveYourScreenTime doesn't use any third-party services, libraries, or SDKs that collect data — no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no A/B-testing tools. There is simply nothing installed in the app that could send your information anywhere, to us or to anyone else.

05

Your control

  • Edit or delete any week's entry at any time, directly in the app
  • Delete the app to permanently erase everything it stored — there's nothing left behind on a server, because there is no server
  • Nothing to export or request from us, because we never had a copy in the first place
06

Children's privacy

SaveYourScreenTime is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone — which, given the app collects no information from any user of any age, applies equally here.

07

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that changes what's described above — this page will be updated and the date at the top will change accordingly.
