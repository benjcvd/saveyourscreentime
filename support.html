Common questions

Why doesn't my total match Settings → Screen Time?

On some devices, Apple's own Screen Time total includes Always-On Display and other system-level time that isn't attributed to any individual app. Your per-app numbers are unaffected — this only shows up as a gap between the total and the sum of your apps, which the app explains right on the review screen when it's large enough to look like a mistake.

How does importing a screenshot work?

Take a screenshot of your Screen Time week view in Settings, then import it. The app reads the text directly on your phone using Apple's on-device text recognition — the photo itself is never uploaded anywhere and is discarded the moment the numbers are read out of it. You always get to review and correct everything before it's saved.

What does Reduce vs. Neutral mean?

Not all screen time is the same — checking a map or your banking app isn't the same as doomscrolling. Reduce is time you're actively trying to cut down; Neutral is time that's unavoidable or fine as-is. You can reclassify any app or category at any time from Trends → the settings icon.

Is my data private?

Yes — everything stays on your device. There's no account, no server, and no analytics of any kind. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

Can I edit or delete a week I already logged?

Yes — tap any week in the Log tab to edit its total or notes, or delete it entirely. Per-app numbers from an imported week aren't individually editable yet; if one needs correcting, delete that week and re-import the screenshot.
